## Brief comum enviado à Image Gen

Create a realistic, production-quality UI concept for RegulaCheck, a Brazilian Portuguese Android app helping a patient prepare documents and resolve administrative paperwork. Generate ONE screen, not a collage, on a natural 390 x 844 mobile viewport, scaled up cleanly for detail. App content only: no phone body, bezel, outer device mask, OS status bar, time, battery, signal, home bar, browser chrome, mockup background or shadows. Crisp perfectly flat digital UI. Current date anchor: 13/11/2026, Brazil.

The attached Headspace Explore iOS reference is the SELECTED visual direction. Capture its calm minimalism, friendly characterful HUMANIST typography, rounded functional surfaces and patient rhythm of whitespace. The user chose this for a bureaucracy helper and explicitly wants FEW illustrations. In these functional screens use NO illustrations, NO mascots, NO stock imagery, NO decorative floating objects. Get the friendly personality from type, composition and reassuring concrete copy. Do not borrow the meditation/courses/gamification or graphics. Do not use the previous generic dashboard idiom of huge document icons, status chips everywhere, big dark cards, gradients, oversize full-width buttons stacked repeatedly, noisy borders. Headspace is not being cloned.

Typography direction: titles use Bricolage Grotesque medium/semibold at 30–34sp, normal width, relaxed and slightly quirky humanist forms, not geometric corporate bold or condensed all caps. Body, metadata and actions use Nunito Sans regular/semibold, body 16sp, metadata 13–14sp. Do not make all words bold. Keep title letter spacing natural and open counters. These are similar in SPIRIT to the reference, not its exact typeface. Max two font families. Keep readable sizes, generous line height and a quiet confident rhythm. Broad 24dp side margins; gaps 8/16/24/32; touch controls 48–56dp tall. Functional corners 20–28dp; CTA may be pill-shaped but not huge. Friendly adult product, no childish or smiley cartoon theme. Layout should feel custom designed and composed, not like default components in a dashboard.

The second attached reference is the USER'S BRAND PALETTE, binding: navy #112B3C for text; petrol blue #205375 for primary actions; orange #F6680E sparingly as a meaningful small signal; white #FFFFFF main base and neutral #EFEFEF for a few grouped surfaces. Existing support tones #526774 muted ink, #EAF2F8 light blue, #FFF1E6 light orange, #8D3C0C readable orange-brown. Respect these colors. Do not replace them with cobalt/electric blue, teal or purple. No large blue hero area; no dark navy request card. Never normal white text on orange. Flat solid colors, no gradients, textures, paper grain, glass or drop shadows.

Use hierarchy, spacing and alignment first. At most ONE softly rounded background container when it genuinely groups a current task; otherwise use whitespace and occasional subtle dividers. No nested cards. Avoid heavy alert treatment or repeated pills: use plain text states with one small recognizable icon or marker. No big left column of generic circular icons. One clear primary action, only necessary supporting actions. All user-visible text correct Brazilian Portuguese, no lorem ipsum. Keep things sparse and uncluttered with realistic content, not empty decorative space or a feature inventory. No fake charts, metrics, search, bottom tabs, notifications, appointments, waiting times or unrelated navigation. Simple back/help controls only when useful.

Domain constraints: academic prototype, all synthetic data. It does not diagnose, define clinical urgency, authorize care or submit to real SUS. Any simulated reading/result must have a visible 'SIMULADO' label. Patient may check transcription but may not change the original clinical document; corrections to clinical content belong to the issuing unit. Do not invent a 'send to unit' action or promise integration with a unit. Readable bottom footer on every screen: 'Protótipo acadêmico · dados fictícios'. This is a visual exploration, not a functioning native app.

Design only the focused screen described next. Keep the SAME visual identity, typography, palette and spacing across the sequence. Fit cleanly inside 390x844 without truncation of essential information.
